## Compatibility

Existing records remain valid: all three events are new, and a session that is not a room carries none of them. A build that does not know them refuses the log rather than reading a discussion without what was said or concluded, which is the intended reading — the transcript is the record a reader came for, and a reader that silently dropped a remark would misreport the discussion. Three roots rather than one because they answer different questions: who is in the room, what one participant said, and how it ended.
